\cvsection{Research}{
  \textbf{Residential Segregation in a Dual Labor Market: Evidence from Bogotá}\\
  \emph{Presented at LACEA 2026}\\[3pt]
  Does the spatial concentration of formal jobs amplify residential segregation in Bogotá?
  Using confidential 2021 Encuesta Multipropósito data and a quantitative spatial model
  of formal and informal work, I study how job access, earnings, rents, and amenities
  shape where workers live. Dispersing formal jobs has little effect on its own; allowing
  amenities to adjust substantially reduces segregation.
}
